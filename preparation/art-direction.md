# Godkänd bildriktning och spelresurser

Referens: det andra godkända bildförslaget, ”Paper Puppet in the Printing Workshop”. Tillverkade papperslager, varm och dämpad palett, handritade profiler och tydliga ansiktsdrag. Första bildförslaget och generiska geometriska figurer används inte som målbild.

Resurser: `dist/assets/scene-atlas.png` och `dist/assets/character-atlas.png`. Båda skapades med det inbyggda bildstödet och den godkända bilden som referens. Bakgrunderna är 2 × 5 lika rutor; figurerna är 5 × 2 rutor med transparent bakgrund. Spelet läser rutorna som bildlager, med separat rörlig spelare och personer.

No gutters, no margins, straight panel edges. Each panel is a complete wide side-view point-and-click stage, objects large/readable. Row 1 left: Swedish 1700s town square, baker stall at x20%, noticeboard x38%, inn x70%, cart x90%, usable empty foreground. Row 1 right: botanical garden, glass greenhouse left, plant beds center, ladder gate right. Row 2 left: Swedish farm and windmill, mill at x15%, fields center, red farmhouse right. Row 2 right: printing workshop closely matching reference, wooden screw hand press centered x38%, type cabinets x75%, table with ink/paper/type trays foreground center-right, empty character spot x70%, doorway far left. Row 3 left: Swedish coffeehouse, tables, cups, shelves, empty foreground. Row 3 right: estate parliament room, four distinct rows of benches, writing desk at x65%, tall windows. Row 4 left: Gothenburg harbor, China trading sailship x80%, crates x40%, warehouse left. Row 4 right: Gustav III palace antechamber, portrait x42%, columns and golden door x82%. Row 5 left: opera theatre backstage, burgundy paper curtains, stage, empty character spot x60%. Row 5 right: Swedish colonial harbor archive on Saint Barthelemy, tropical port seen beyond a writing table and ledger; show no enslaved people or exotic caricatures. No lettering, labels or panel numbers.

## Prompt: figurer

Create a GAME CHARACTER SPRITE ATLAS matching the provided approved paper puppet reference exactly: expressive hand-drawn profile faces with prominent noses, tactile cut rag-paper clothing, visible warm paper grain, layered cutout pieces. TRANSPARENT BACKGROUND true alpha, no scenery, no lettering, no grid lines. Exact 5 columns x 2 rows, TEN equal rectangular cells, one full-body character centered in each cell, same scale, feet at 94% cell height, head at 8%, all fully inside own cell with generous transparent margin. All stand in three-quarter profile facing RIGHT with a relaxed talking hand gesture; they will be mirrored by game code, no walking cycle needed. Row1 col1: young apprentice/player, blue-green waistcoat, cream shirt, brown breeches, dark shoes, brown hair tied with ribbon, no apron. Row1 col2: baker Marta, middle-aged woman in cream cap and ochre dress/apron, flour on sleeve. Row1 col3: botanist Sam, green coat, spectacles, holding small plant sprig. Row1 col4: farmer Karin, rust-red skirt, blue bodice, cream cap, sturdy silhouette. Row1 col5: printer Brita, woman with brown tied-back hair, rolled cream sleeves, dark waistcoat, long ink-stained apron; resembles the cut-paper craft of reference. Row2 col1: letter writer Noor, dark brown complexion, brown coat and cream collar, folded letter. Row2 col2: parliament scribe Elias, narrow face, glasses, dark plum coat, paper/quill. Row2 col3: harbor clerk Jo, broad face, blue coat, tricorn hat, ledger. Row2 col4: palace messenger Amira, brown complexion, burgundy coat, tied hair, sealed letter. Row2 col5: teacher Rut, older woman, grey hair, slate-blue dress, small book. Each distinct recognizable human silhouette, drawn friendly but not infantile. Match the reference's well-observed clothing folds, cut-paper texture and expressive profile drawing, NOT simplistic geometric icons, NOT shiny 3D, NOT flat vector sticker outlines. Keep every cell exactly equal and no character crossing its cell boundary. Output wide atlas ideally 2560x1536.

Bilderna är stiliserade spelmiljöer, inte historiska källor eller en exakt rekonstruktion. Figurarket följer inte alla efterfrågade persondetaljer helt. Särskilt porträtt, golvkontakt och föremålsplacering behöver fortsatt visuell provspelning.

## Uppdatering: flatare kulisser och samtal

Bakgrundsarket har ritats om med den godkända referensen och det tidigare arket: dämpad gråsalvia, ockra, gråblått och gräddvitt; tydliga klippta lager, grund scen och enklare former. Nedersta tredjedelen lämnas fri som spelyta. Tryckeriets stora förgrundsbord har tagits bort. Personernas höjd och golvkontakt anges nu i samma scenkoordinater, de vänder sig mot spelaren och samtal visas i två separata serierutor. Fem frivilliga besökare samtalar utan poäng eller resurskostnad.
